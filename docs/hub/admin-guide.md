# Volunteer Hub — guide for administrators and organisers

The Hub answers: **what's on, who's expected, who's offered to help, who has actually agreed, who to
contact, and where help is needed.** Nothing is ever sent just because you saved something.

## The four kinds of involvement (never mixed up)
| Shown as | Means | How it happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirmed to help** | They agreed to a specific request | They accepted in the Hub, or you recorded their offline yes ("recorded by organiser") |
| **Venue-wide cover** | One person covering a venue for a time (café, opening up) | Arranged once in *Venue cover*; shown on every overlapping event |
| **Expected by role** | Linked to the team (manager, coach) | From their team role; **not** confirmed |
| **Available to help** | Said "yes, I could help" | Their reply to "Can you help?"; **not** a booking |
Organisers also see **Awaiting response** (asked, not replied, or invited and not answered) and **Declined**.

## People
- *Manage → People → + Person*: a name is enough. Email and phone are optional.
- Two separate switches, both **off** by default: **Send portal invitation now** (needs an email) and **Include in suggested availability recipients** (only pre-ticks them later).
- Invite someone later from their record: *Portal access → Send portal invitation*. If email is off or fails, it says **Invitation not sent** — nothing pretends it worked.
- Teams: add a team role (Manager, Coach…) and tick **Expected at this team's events**. End a role with an end date; history stays.
- Contact details are hidden from members unless the person chooses to share them. Under-18s are never listed to members and can't be invited in this version.
- *Remove their details (on request)*: for a leaver who asks to be forgotten. Irreversible; events keep "Former volunteer".

## Events
- *+ Event*: name, date, start, finish (leave blank if unknown — we never guess), where (Marke Wood, or type an away/outside location). One switch: **Ask volunteers for availability?**
- Freemens Way is kept but inactive: its old events stay; it isn't offered for new ones (Settings → Venues to reactivate).
- After saving: **Request availability**, **Invite a person**, **Assign shared venue cover** — all optional.
- Changing the date, time or place asks anyone confirmed to reconfirm. Cancelling needs a reason; the event stays on the calendar.
- *Copy to another date* makes a new draft with the same details — no invitations, replies or cover are copied.

## Asking for help
- **Request availability** (event page): people who usually get asked are pre-ticked; review, then **Send requests**. Only the people you asked can show as "not replied".
- **Invite a person**: choose someone (anyone in the directory, even if they don't get routine requests), an optional responsibility (e.g. Matchday support) and times. *Send request* (they accept/decline) or *Record that they've already agreed*.
- People without a Hub login: contact them yourself, then use **Record: they said yes/no** or **Record someone's availability**. It shows as recorded by you.
- *Manage → Requests* lists everything still waiting.

## Shared venue cover
*Manage → Venue cover*: date, times, venue, person, responsibility. **Show overlapping events** previews what it will sit alongside. Saved once; change or cancel it here and every event updates. If the person is already needed somewhere else at the same time, you'll get a clash warning — change the times or save with a note.
For an event with no recorded finish, cover is only shown as **at kick-off**.

## Club roles
*Manage → Club roles*: who holds what (one person can hold several; a role can be shared). **Recruiting** is separate from **who holds it**: a filled role can look for a successor or extra help without removing the holder. Tick **Can appear on the public volunteer page** for roles you want advertised; only recruiting roles appear there, without any names or contact details. Track interest: interested → contacted → discussion → shadowing → handover. Nothing here is a formal club or FA appointment.

## Applications (public form)
*Manage → Applications*: offers of help from `/volunteer/apply`. Applicants have no login and haven't been invited. Each shows their answers, a **possible duplicate** flag (compare side by side before linking — nothing is merged automatically), and **suggested roles** with the reasons. Move them through New → In review → Contacted → Trial → Accepted into pool → Closed, set a follow-up date, add notes. **Add to directory** creates a person (no login, no invite); invite them from their record if wanted. Delete on request.

## Settings (administrators)
Club contact email (shown on the public pages), accept applications on/off, application review period (when the club agrees one), day-before reminders, email start/pause, venues, teams, and CSV downloads. Download, store securely, delete old copies.
