# Volunteer Hub — guide for volunteers

**Signing in:** go to the Hub, enter the email the club invited, and type the 6-digit code we email you.
No passwords.

**Home** shows anything waiting for you:
- **You've been asked to help** — tap **Accept** or **Decline**. You're only booked once you accept.
- **Can you help?** — the club asked who could help at an event. Tap **Reply**, then **Yes** (whole event, or the times you're free) or **No**. Saying yes isn't a booking; an organiser will confirm with you.
- **You're helping** — what you've agreed to. If you can no longer do it, open it and tap **I can no longer do this**.

**Calendar** lists every club event — home and away, matches, training, meetings, socials. Events marked
**Help wanted** let you answer "Can you help?". Others are for information.

**People** is the club directory: names, teams and club roles. Contact details only appear if that person
chose to share them.

**Roles** shows club roles looking for help or a successor. Tap **I'm interested** and someone will be in touch.

**Me (My Profile):** change the name shown, your phone number, whether other members can see your email or
phone, what you'd enjoy helping with, and when you're usually free (a guide only — it never signs you up).

**Emails** only come when someone asks you to help, when something you agreed to changes, and a reminder
the day before. Not for anything else.

Questions or want your details removed? Contact the club secretary.
